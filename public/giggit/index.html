About this pilot

Giggit is Musty’s gig-work platform for Saint Lucia. This is the MVP test build — Castries only, English only for now (French and Spanish coming), one payment channel.

How it works: a customer posts a gig — with a photo of the problem when it helps. Giggit finds the closest Gigger in the pool by location, like Uber, and pings them the job. A Gigger accepts — the money goes into escrow (held). The Gigger does the work, marks it done, and the customer confirms with the completion code. The money releases after a 1-hour dispute window. Commission is 15% standard, 10% at 20+ gigs a month, 5% for Giggit Pro.

Money in this test build is simulated. The Giggit Wallet is a practice ledger — no real money moves. Real payments (Mona, WiPay, bank transfers, cash QR) come in a later phase.

Notifications: use the button below to turn on push alerts for new gigs. On iPhone, web notifications only work if you add Giggit to your Home Screen first (iOS 16.4+). SMS alerts are coming with the SMS gateway.

Built by TAIG for Musty. Giggit is Musty’s platform — TAIG builds it and runs it; Musty pays the infrastructure at cost when money comes in.
