Hereditary numbers.

What 2025 inherited — and what it broke.

Hereditary numbers are the numbers a season does not start from zero with: the record, the affiliations, the park, and the recent failures a team carries into Opening Day. They are the honest baseline for judging whether a title is a sudden spike or a return to something the place already knows how to do.

Five seasons, on paper

Jacksonville has been the Miami Marlins' affiliate since 2009 and a Triple-A club since 2021, playing at VyStar Ballpark. Under manager David Carpenter, in his first season in the role, the 2025 team reversed the exact thing that had sunk the two teams before it.

In 2023, Jacksonville scored plenty and still lost, because 825 runs allowed overwhelmed everything. In 2024, the scoring fell to 652 and the prevention only partly recovered. In 2025, scoring recovered modestly to 693 while runs allowed dropped to 607 — a 92-run improvement on 2024 and a 218-run improvement on 2023. The hereditary problem was run prevention. The 2025 team, by the numbers

A team that wins 89 games with this offensive profile is not winning the obvious way.

Team slash

.243 / .330 / .402

.732 OPS as a team, with 162 home runs and 653 runs batted in. Batting average, on-base, slugging, and runs per game all ranked in the bottom third of Triple-A — roughly 25th to 28th of 30 teams.

Prevention

607 allowed

4.0 runs allowed per game late in the season — the second-fewest in Triple-A — and among the level's best at limiting hits for most of the year.

Both halves

47–28 · 42–33

First half .627, second half .560. The second half was weaker — this report does not hide that — but it was still a winning half, after the playoff berth was already secured, in a league where rosters turn over constantly.

Home and road

44–30 · 45–31

Home .595, road .592. Almost no split to explain away — one more road win than home win. Whatever VyStar Ballpark gave them, they took the same baseball on the road.

The crowd

359,679 fans

A record crowd in the first season after the Project NEXT renovations at VyStar Ballpark.

History

8th title

The 2025 club won the city's eighth league title in the Double-A/Triple-A era (1962–present) — the first International League title since 1968, when the club was a New York Mets affiliate. The most recent title of any kind before 2025 was 2014, in the Southern League. The 89 wins are the most for a Jacksonville club since 1964 and, per the club, tied for the fourth-highest win total in franchise history.
