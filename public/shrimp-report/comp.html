Framed in the pitch

The 2024 Dodgers, repeated 2025

The comp club as framed: the 2024 Dodgers, who repeated in 2025. What they did to get a championship, and what they did to sustain it — that is what the Shrimp are held up against. The pick itself is Tee's call, and it stays open on this report.

The test a comp has to pass

Development, not payroll

The Dodgers are a spend-money case. The Shrimp and the Marlins are a development organization. A comp that only teaches payroll teaches the Shrimp nothing they can use. The comp has to teach development — what the club did to get players, grow them, and keep the pipeline full — or it is the wrong comp.

The MLB sustain case

The Brewers, on TSR

On the full TSR, baseball's case is the Brewers, not the Dodgers — too easy, just spend money. The Brewers stay the MLB sustain case. The Shrimp's own comp pick is separate from that, and it stays open.

What the comp is for

Three questions the comp answers — once the pick is locked.

1. What did they do to get it? Not what they spent — what they did. The moves, the development, the pattern behind the championship.

2. What did they do to sustain it? Getting great is one problem. Staying great is the other. The comp has to show both, or it is only half a comp.

3. What do the Shrimp draft and develop to do the same? Development leads, draft follows. The comp's answer has to land in the Shrimp's lane — records, playoffs, chips, and the pipeline into the majors.

Until the pick is locked, this page stays as framing. Nothing here names a comp as decided.
