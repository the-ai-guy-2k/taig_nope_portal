The test, stated now.

Five tests. Filed Nov 25, 2026. Scored in public.

Sustain greatness does not mean repeating 89–61 exactly. At Triple-A, rosters are rebuilt by the parent club every winter, and holding any team to the same win total with a different roster would be a dishonest test. It means the way Jacksonville won — prevention first, depth over dependence, competitive in every setting — survives the roster turnover.

Five tests, filed Nov 25, 2026

Stated here in terms that can be scored after the 2026 season without moving the goalposts. If three or more of the five hold, the 2025 championship reads as the start of a sustained level. If prevention and the differential both go, it reads as a great team that was assembled once and dispersed — honored here, but not sustained. Either result will be reported on the same terms stated here.

  1. Prevention holds. If Jacksonville allows runs at a rate in the top third of Triple-A again in 2026, the 2025 prevention read was a system, not a staff that happened to be together for one summer. If prevention falls to the middle or bottom of the level while the win total holds up on offense instead, this read was wrong about what carried the team.
  2. Close games regress, but do not collapse. Nobody should expect another .674 in one-run games. A sustain result is a winning one-run record — anything above .500 — alongside a winning record in games decided by three or fewer. A losing one-run record in 2026 would mark the 2025 edge as mostly sequencing.
  3. Both settings, again. A home record built on VyStar Ballpark with a losing road record would break the most proven part of the 2025 pattern. Sustain means a winning road record, or within a few games of one, for a second straight year.
  4. Replacement production appears. Berry, Mack, Marsee, De Los Santos, and Acosta will not all be Jacksonville regulars for another full season — that is the nature of a Marlins affiliate one step from the majors. Sustain means new regulars replace most of that spread production: no single player needing to replace all of it, and the team on-base and stolen-base identity surviving the names changing.
  5. The differential stays positive. The cleanest single check: a second straight positive run differential after +86 in 2025, following −47 in 2024 and −74 in 2023. Two positive years in a row would be the first clear break from the inherited pattern of the losing seasons.

The open call

Score now, or keep forward?

The five tests above were filed to be scored after 2026. The season is over. Whether this report scores them against the real 73–74 now, or keeps the forward read, is Tee's call — it is pending. Nothing on this page marks a test hit or miss until he says so.

A pattern read that cannot be proven wrong is not analysis; it is promotion, and this is not that. Jacksonville has the harder and better problem now. Plenty of teams have had one September. The 2025 Shrimp earned the right to be measured by what comes next — by whether the numbers they inherited, fixed, and finally proved can be proven again.
