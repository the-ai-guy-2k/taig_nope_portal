What it is

The build, as a system

Frame the build as a system: construction spend in — vendor work + new commerce + new people out. A new stadium brings new commerce, new citizens, new people around it — a new set of all of that. The build brings the life; the life brings the work.

Honest state

The capitalizing lanes

Three lanes, already set: vendor doors — getting in the door with the vendors working the build; JSEB — the Jacksonville Small and Emerging Business program; and second-tier work off the Jacksonville build — the work underneath the big work. The COTT filter applies: small money now with potential to grow — NOT prime contracts / big-ticket bids.

Win condition

What counts

Vendor doors opening. JSEB standing. Second-tier work landing — small money now with potential to grow. This lane is about the build and the commerce and people it brings, in that order. All active Jaguars pitches are ON HOLD — this is capitalizing on the build, not a pitch to the Jaguars.

Year-5 number

No separate number

The stadium lane carries no separate year-5 number and no return promise. It is an opportunity lane: the mechanism is vendor doors, second-tier work, and new commerce — not a return guarantee. No guaranteed return figures anywhere on this page.
